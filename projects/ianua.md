# IANUA

**Status:** Active — platform + case studies  
**Repos:** https://github.com/IRsoctierDT/IANUA  
**Docs:** https://irsoctierdt.github.io/IANUA/

Local-first AI operations and cyber command center: agentic triage, MITRE ATT&CK mapping, RAG-backed analysis, policy-gated tool surfaces, tamper-evident auditing, and compliance/trust views. Built to show that SOC automation can be secure-by-default and operable without surrendering control plane to a SaaS black box.
