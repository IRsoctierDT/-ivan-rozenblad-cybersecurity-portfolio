About

Emeryville / San Francisco Bay Area · open to Security Engineer, Detection/SOC, and AI Security roles · consulting welcome.

I came to security the operator’s way — networks, systems, and consulting work that has to survive production. That left a hard rule: security only works when it’s engineered in, not bolted on. Controls need design, documentation, tests, and an audit trail, or they don’t last.

I ship on both sides of the AI/security boundary: using agents to scale defensive ops, and securing the agent/MCP ecosystems organizations are adopting. Full philosophy and roadmap live in the professional statement.

  • Defense in depth, least privilege by defaultAssume a layer fails. Segment, allow-list, default-deny.
  • If it isn’t documented and testable, it isn’t a controlThreat models, ADRs, CI gates, reproducible evidence.
  • Humans approve; machines assistAutomation recommends and prioritizes — people authorize irreversible actions.
